🚀 How To Test the Extension

Ready to see SafeAI in action? Follow these simple steps to verify the privacy protection:

01. Open your favorite AI platform, such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

02. In the message box, try typing something sensitive. For example:
"My private email is test@company.com and my phone number is 123-456-7890."

03. Notice the Warning: As you type, a thin orange line or a warning box will appear above your message, alerting you that sensitive data was detected.

04. Anonymize with One Click: Click the "Fix (Anonymize)" button. Watch as your private details are instantly replaced with safe placeholders like [EMAIL_1].

Document Wizard

Need to analyze a whole file? Use the Document Wizard to safely scan PDFs and documents for sensitive data before uploading them to AI tools.

💡 Pro Tips

• Stay Connected: Ensure the icon in the panel says "Connected". This ensures your company's privacy policies are active.

• Use Side-Panel: Click the SafeAI extension icon in your toolbar to keep the analysis results visible while you work.

• Keyboard Shortcut: Press Ctrl+Shift+S to quickly open or close the SafeAI panel.
