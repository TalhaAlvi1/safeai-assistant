No-Training Commitment

SafeAI explicitly commits that client content processed through our assistant is never used to train public AI models. We provide a secure interface that intercepts and anonymizes data before it reaches LLM providers.

Data Minimization (GDPR/HIPAA Ready)

We only process content you pass through AI tools. No system-wide scanning.

Our architecture follows "Security by Design" principles:

  • Masking of sensitive PII/PHI (Health Info, IDs, Payments) occurs in real-time.
  • No raw content is stored in our long-term logs—only masked summaries and statistics.
  • Tenants have full rights to export or purge their data at any time.

High-Sensitivity Governance

Health info, IDs, and payment data are blocked by default.

Firms can configure strict enforcement policies for these categories to prevent accidental leaks of highly sensitive regulatory data.
